Murray–Darling’s River Pulse

Every river, every day since July 2025

Height is that day’s discharge. Width is the river’s monthly mean.
Colour is water temperature.

BoM daily mean discharge, level and temperature, using midnight-to-midnight days in each station’s standard time, carried along nearby river reaches. Between gauges, values are estimates. Playback interpolates smoothly between daily readings; missing days stay blank. Grey-blue reaches have no temperature reading; width uses the available monthly mean. The timeline stops at the last interval with at least 90% of peak gauge coverage.
